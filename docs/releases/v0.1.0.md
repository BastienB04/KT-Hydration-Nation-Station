Ribbit is ready to remind you to drink water. This first release includes local hydration logging, daily progress, seven-day history, streaks, Snooze/Pause, optional Ribbit sound, and System/Light/Dark themes.

[Download the Windows x64 installer](https://github.com/BastienB04/KT-Hydration-Nation-Station/releases/latest/download/KT-Hydration-Nation-Station-setup.exe), then follow the [installation instructions](https://github.com/BastienB04/KT-Hydration-Nation-Station#install-on-windows). Windows 11 was tested on a physical x64 laptop; Windows 10 21H2 or later is an intended compatibility target and has not been physically validated.

The installer uses your Windows account and requires no administrator elevation for the app. It is not Authenticode-signed, so SmartScreen may show **Unknown publisher**. For this official download, use **More info → Run anyway**; keep SmartScreen and Defender enabled.

Closing the window leaves Ribbit running in the notification area, sometimes under **^**. Use the tray menu to quit. Live notification clicks open Pond; old Notification Center items and notifications left after quitting cannot open the app.

Future updates are verified with the app's updater signing key. Ribbit checks on launch and every six hours when enabled. Download an update, then choose **Restart now** or **Later**. Settings and hydration history are kept.

No accounts, telemetry, cloud storage, or recurring fee. Your hydration data stays on your computer. Optional update checks and downloads contact GitHub without sending your history or settings.
